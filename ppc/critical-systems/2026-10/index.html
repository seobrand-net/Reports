01Notes

Reading this report

Two short notes before the numbers.

Conversions

Every conversion is a lead

Conversions are leads from an ad click, as Google Ads counts them: lead forms submitted on the website and calls from the ads or from the website.

Schedule

Ads run on weekdays

The ads run Monday to Friday, so weekend days show no activity.
