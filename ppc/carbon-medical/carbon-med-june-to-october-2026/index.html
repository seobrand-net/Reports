01Notes

Reading this report

Two short notes before the numbers.

Total impression share

How often the ads showed

Total impression share is the percentage of eligible searches where the ad showed, in any position on the page. It changes a lot from month to month, so it appears only in the Month Comparison section. Google reports any share under 10% as “<10%”.

Reporting period

One total since launch

Every figure above the Month Comparison section is the total for June 22 – October 31, 2026, treated as one period. June 22 is the campaign's first day with impressions, so there is no earlier period to compare against.
