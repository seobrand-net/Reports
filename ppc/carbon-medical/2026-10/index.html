Google Ads: the Durasphere ads showed 394 times in October, in line with September's 401. They appeared in 64.5% of eligible Durasphere searches and as the very first ad in 51.1%, up from 50.1% in September. They also drew 42 clicks, up from 22, and spend was $356.30.

01Notes

Reading this report

Two short notes before the numbers.

Total impression share

How often the ads showed

Total impression share is the percentage of eligible searches where the ad showed, in any position on the page. Google reports any share under 10% as “<10%”.

Impression share (absolute top)

How often the ad was the very first ad

Impression share (absolute top) counts only the times the ad showed as the very first ad above the organic search results, as a percentage of all eligible searches. It is always equal to or lower than the total impression share.
