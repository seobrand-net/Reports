01Notes

Reading this report

Two short notes before the numbers.

Structure

Three parts

This report has three parts: Google Ads (sections 02 to 06), Local Services Ads (07) and CallRail (08). Each part uses only its own data.

Call status

Answered, not answered, first-time and repeat

Answered means someone picked up the call, including calls that went to voicemail. Not answered means the caller hung up before anyone picked up, or the call was missed. First-time means it was the first call CallRail recorded from that number; Repeat means it had recorded one before.
