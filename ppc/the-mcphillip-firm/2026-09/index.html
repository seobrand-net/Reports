Google Ads spend

September spend was $4,321, 2.5% less than August, and clicks rose 16% to 940, but Google Ads conversions fell from 51 to 41, so cost per conversion rose 21% to $105.39. In CallRail, Google Ads leads fell from 78 to 42 (41 calls and 1 form) while Local Services Ads calls rose from 6 to 17. Calls from the ads came mostly in the first half: 37 of the 41 Google Ads calls in CallRail arrived before September 16.

The focus now: With the call extension paused on Sep 15 and the location asset removed on Sep 25, every ad call now goes through the website. The account had no conversions from Sep 24 to 29, so October shows whether the website path brings the calls back.
01Notes

Reading this report

What each number means and where it comes from.

Two counts

Why Google Ads shows 41 conversions and CallRail 42 leads

Google Ads counts the calls and forms it can tie to an ad: 31 calls placed from the ad itself, 8 first-time calls to the website tracking number and 2 form submissions. CallRail counts every call that came in through a Google Ads tracking number, plus the website form.

From September 16 to 30, Google Ads logged 22 calls placed from the ads that rang the Google Business Profile number, which CallRail files under Google My Business. Those calls are in the Google Ads conversions but not in the CallRail lead count, so CallRail understates the second half of the month.

Call routing

What changed in September

On September 15 the call extension was paused across the account, so ad clicks lead to the website, where CallRail tracks the calls. Until September 25 the location asset still showed a call button with the Business Profile number; it was removed that day.

Google Ads logged no calls from ads between September 24 and 30. Demand Gen - General started on September 26, which explains most of the late-month jump in impressions and clicks; it had no conversions in September.

Local Services Ads

Local Services Ads are reported separately

Local Services Ads run in a separate Google account, so their spend is not part of the $4,321 in this report. Their 17 calls appear in the Lead Report under Google Local Services Ads.

Cost per lead divides Google Ads spend by Google Ads leads only (42), leaving the Local Services Ads calls out.

Forms

Where the form count comes from

CallRail does not track website forms for this site. Forms come from Google Analytics: thank-you page views from Google Ads traffic. There was 1 in September, on Sep 16 from PMax - General.

Google Ads counted 2 Lead Form Submission conversions. Google dates a conversion by the ad click, so the two counts can differ by a form submitted days after the click.

Call status

Answered and not answered

Answered includes calls that went to voicemail. Not answered means the caller hung up before anyone picked up, or the call was missed. In September 9 of the 58 paid calls were not answered (15.5%), against 7 of 80 in August.

First-time means the number had never called the firm before; Repeat means it had.

Impression share

How visible the ads were

Impression share is the percentage of eligible searches where an ad showed. The rest was lost for one of two reasons: budget (the daily budget ran out) or rank (the bid or ad quality was too low to win the auction).

CallRail began tracking Google Ads calls in May 2026, so the month comparison shows leads from May onward only.
