Leads Under $50K

In October, Meta Ads brought in 24 forms filled over $50K, up from 21 in September, from $3,870.22 in spend and 848 link clicks. The cost per form over $50K fell to $161.26 from $179.14. In the website lead sheet, 29 leads came from Meta Ads clicks: 16 over $50K and 13 under $50K.

01Notes

Reading this report

Two short notes before the numbers.

Forms over $50K

Forms filled for vehicles over $50K

Forms over $50K are the forms submitted for a vehicle worth more than $50,000, the result the campaigns optimize for in Meta Ads.

Two counts

Meta Ads forms and website leads

Part 1 counts forms over $50K as Meta Ads reports them. Part 2 lists every lead in the website lead sheet that came from a Meta Ads click, over and under $50K. Meta Ads also counts people who clicked an ad and came back to the site another way, so its count of forms over $50K is higher than the leads over $50K in the sheet.
